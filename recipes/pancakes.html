Classic Pancakes

Description

These classic pancakes are fluffy, delicious, and easy to make. Perfect for breakfast or brunch, they can be customized with your favorite toppings. This recipe produces light, tender pancakes with a hint of vanilla flavor, making them a family favorite.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter (plus more for cooking)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla (if using) until smooth.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, stirring until just combined.
  4. Preheat a skillet and lightly coat it with butter. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake onto the skillet.
  5. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden brown. Serve with desired toppings.
