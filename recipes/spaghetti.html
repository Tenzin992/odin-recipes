Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Description

Spaghetti Aglio e Olio is a simple and flavorful pasta dish, perfect for a quick, comforting meal. Made with garlic, olive oil, and red pepper flakes, this Italian classic brings out delicious flavors with just a few ingredients. It's a great option for busy nights or when you want something warm and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and sauté the sliced garlic until golden brown.
  3. Add red pepper flakes, if using, and cook briefly.
  4. Combine the drained pasta with the garlic oil mixture, adding a splash of reserved pasta water if needed.
  5. Season with salt and black pepper. Garnish with chopped parsley and grated Parmesan.
